About Marginalia

I read a lot of research papers and I often find them to be hard to understand at first. This is where I write what took to actually get here.

Why this exists?

Most online breakdowns either oversimplify to the paper to the point of being useless, or just restate the abstract in different words. I wanted to create a platform that somewhere in between. The actual math works out. The parts that take me 3 rereads are well explained. Frankly,I'm stil not 100% sure about many parts

I want this website to serve as a platform where I can document my notes for the paper instead of messy notebooks.

It started as a one-page website and it has grown to a complex website with a remarkable architecture.

What's on here

  • Margin Notes: Definitions, Key Takeaways and Notation
  • Real Math:Equations → Rendered through Katex.
  • A small archive:Papers are searchable and filterable by topics.

How it's built

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS on the frontend and Supabase database.
